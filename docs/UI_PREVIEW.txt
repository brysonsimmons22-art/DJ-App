INTERACTION PATTERNS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. TAP PLAY BUTTON
   State: Paused → Playing
   Effect: Vinyl starts rotating, waveform animates

2. SWIPE LEFT (on Now Playing)
   Action: Skip to next track
   Visual: Slide animation, new vinyl spins in

3. DRAG PLAYLIST ITEM
   Action: Reorder songs
   Visual: Item lifts, others shift, compatibility updates

4. LONG PRESS TRACK
   Action: Show options menu
   Options: Remove, Move to top, View details

5. PULL DOWN (on Playlist)
   Action: Refresh from streaming service
   Visual: Loading spinner, "Analyzing tracks..."
